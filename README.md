# 450+ HR & Recruiter Contacts for Job and Internship Search

A preview of an India-focused HR and recruiter contact directory for students, fresh graduates, and job seekers exploring internships and off-campus opportunities.

**Full directory:** 462 recruiter records in the source dataset  
**Preview below:** 20 sample records  
**Format:** Digital directory with recruiter names, roles, companies, email addresses, and LinkedIn profile links where available.

> **Get the complete directory:** [Visit Codemasters – Full 450+ HR & Recruiter Directory](https://rebrand.ly/codeMasters7)

---

## What you'll find

The directory is intended to help job seekers organise their recruiter research and identify professional contacts to approach about relevant opportunities.

Depending on the record, fields include:

- Recruiter or HR contact name
- Job title or HR role
- Company name
- Professional email address, where listed
- LinkedIn profile, where listed
- Country

- Contact people only for relevant professional purposes, respect their preferences, and comply with applicable privacy, marketing, and platform rules.
